Learn about the GCSE grading scale

GCSEs in England have been graded on a 9 to 1 scale since 2017. This replaced the previous A* to G scale.

The number scale is not directly equivalent to the old letter one. But the scales are comparable at:

  • the bottom of grade 7 ≈ old grade A
  • the bottom of grade 4 ≈ old grade C
  • the bottom of grade 1 ≈ old grade G
