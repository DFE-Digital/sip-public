Attainment 8

Attainment 8 is a score based on a pupil’s results in 8 GCSE-level subjects.

Subjects included

The 8 subjects that are used in Attainment 8 are split into the following subject areas:

  • English
  • maths
  • 3 Ebacc subjects - a group of GCSE subjects that help students gain a broad set of knowledge and skills including sciences, geography, history and languages
  • 3 other subjects from a group approved by the Department for Education including arts and technical subjects

How the score is calculated

Each GCSE grade is given a point score. For example, grade 9 is equivalent to 9 points, grade 8 is equilavent to 8 points, and so on.

The points from a pupil’s 8 best subjects are added together to give their Attainment 8 score.

English and maths are double weighted, so they each count twice in the total score.

For example, a grade 7 in English is worth 7 points. Because English is double weighted, it contributes 14 points to the total score.

How this works in practice

Pupils do not take specific subjects for Attainment 8. Instead, their 8 best results are used, as long as they fit into the groups above.

This approach encourages schools to offer a broad curriculum, while recognising pupils’ strengths across different subjects.
