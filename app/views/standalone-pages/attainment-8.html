Attainment 8

Attainment 8 is a score based on a pupil’s results in 8 GCSE-level subjects.

Subjects included

Attainment 8 is made up of different subject areas. These help make sure pupils study a broad and balanced range of subjects.

They include:

  • English
  • Maths
  • EBacc subjects – up to 3 subjects from a group that includes sciences, computer science, geography, history and languages
  • Other approved subjects – up to 3 subjects from a wider range of GCSEs or vocational qualifications, including arts and technical subjects

Together, these make up 8 subjects in total.

How this works in practice

Pupils do not have to take specific subjects for Attainment 8. Instead, their 8 best results are used, as long as they fit into the groups above.

This approach encourages schools to offer a broad curriculum, while recognising pupils’ strengths across different subjects.

How the score is calculated

Each GCSE grade is given a point score. The points from a pupil’s 8 best subjects are added together to give their Attainment 8 score.

English and maths are double weighted, so they each count twice in the total score.

For example, a grade 7 in English is worth 7 points. Because English is double weighted, it contributes 14 points to the total score.
