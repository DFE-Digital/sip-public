Pupil characteristics

Disadvantaged pupils

Pupils are identified as disadvantaged if any of the following apply:

  • they were eligible for free school meals at any time during the last 6 years
  • they have been in the care of the local council for a day or more
  • they have been adopted from care

Pupils with English as an additional language

Pupils who hear or use a language other than English at home.

This does not show how well a child speaks English and does not mean they are new to the UK.

Non-mobile pupils

Non-mobile pupils are pupils who stayed at the same school for their final 2 years of secondary school, years 10 and 11.

Prior attainment

Prior attainment shows the level pupils reached at the end of the previous key stage. Understanding pupils’ starting point helps to understand how much they have progressed.

There is no Key Stage 4 prior attainment data for the academic years ending in 2025 and 2026. Due to the COVID-19 disruption, there was no Key Stage 2 data for these pupils, so progress could not be tracked.

Low prior attainers

Pupils who had lower levels of attainment at the end of the previous key stage.

Middle prior attainers

Pupils who achieved around the expected level at the end of the previous key stage.

High prior attainers

Pupils who had high levels of attainment at the end of the previous key stage.
