Pupil characteristics

Disadvantaged pupils

Pupils are identified as disadvantaged if any of the following apply:

  • they were eligible for free school meals at any time during the last 6 years
  • they have been in the care of the local council for a day or more
  • they have been adopted from care

Pupils with English as an additional language

Pupils who hear or use a language other than English at home.

This does not show how well a child speaks English and does not mean they are new to the UK.

Non-mobile pupils

Pupils who stayed at the same school for their final 2 years of secondary school (years 10 and 11).

Prior attainment

Prior attainment is a score based on pupils’ previous attainment.

For pupils in Key Stage 4, prior attainment is based on their Key Stage 2 attainment in:

  • English reading
  • maths national curriculum tests

Understanding pupils’ prior attainment helps to show how much they have progressed.

Pupils can achieve a score between 90 to 120.

Low prior attainers

Pupils whose average score was below 100.

Middle prior attainers

Pupils whose average score was greater than or equal to 100 but less than 110.

High prior attainers

Pupils whose average score was greater than or equal to 110.

There is no Key Stage 4 prior attainment data for the academic years ending in 2025 and 2026.

This is because there is no Key Stage 2 prior attainment data available for these pupils due to COVID-19 disruption.
