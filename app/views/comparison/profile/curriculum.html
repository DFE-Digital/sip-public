Curriculum

A school’s curriculum sets out the subjects, knowledge, skills and experiences it provides to pupils, aiming to give them a broad and balanced education.

Learn more about curriculums at key stage 3 and 4

Key stage 3 (KS3)

KS3 is for pupils usually aged 11 to 14. In KS3 pupils study the core subjects of English, maths and science, and the national curriculum’s foundation subjects. Schools can also include other subjects or topics in their curriculum.

Key stage 4 (KS4)

Key stage 4 (KS4) qualifications in England are usually taken by pupils aged 14 to 16. Pupils usually take a combination of:

  • compulsory core subject GCSEs such as English, maths and science
  • additional subject GCSEs such as art, languages, history and geography
  • vocational subject qualifications such as BTECs

While most pupils must take core subject GCSEs, other subjects depend on pupil preference and the school’s curriculum.

What you could ask schools about the subjects they offer

When researching, contacting or visiting a school, you may want to ask:

  • what is the balance of compulsory and optional GCSEs that pupils can take?
  • which qualifications could my child take?
  • what is the maximum and minimum qualifications a pupil can take at the school?
  • what are the most popular subjects?
  • how do you decide the right number of GCSEs for each pupil to enter?
  • are there limited spaces for pupils on optional subjects?
