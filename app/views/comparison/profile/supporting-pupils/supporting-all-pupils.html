Policies and pupil support

School policies explain the school’s values and what it is responsible for. They also set out what the school expects from pupils and families.

Pastoral care

Pastoral care is the framework a school uses to support the social, emotional and physical well-being of all pupils, so every child feels safe and supported.

Pastoral care varies between schools, but can include support with behaviour, access to counselling, calm spaces and support for children with health conditions.

quiz

What you could ask schools about pastoral care

When researching, contacting or visiting a school, you may want to ask:

  • how does the school create a sense of belonging for pupils?
  • what support is available to each year group?
  • how does the school build strong collaborative relationships with parents and carers?
  • how does the school support pupils who may be more vulnerable or need individual support?

Behaviour policies

When you choose a school, you agree to follow its behaviour policy, so it’s important that it suits your child.

Schools approach behaviour in different ways. A school’s behaviour policy explains how it encourages positive behaviour and keeps the learning environment safe and productive.

Visiting a school can help you see whether pupils are engaged and feel that they belong, which can help encourage positive behaviour.

What you could ask schools about the behaviour policy

When researching, contacting or visiting a school, you may want to ask:

  • how does the school check that it applies the policy fairly and as intended?
  • what training or coaching do staff get on behaviour?
  • how does the school respond to misbehaviour or reward good behaviour?
  • what does the school consider bullying and how is it dealt with?
  • how will the school communicate with parents about their child’s behaviour?
