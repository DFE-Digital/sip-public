Attendance

2024 to 2025 academic year

Schools must record pupil attendance twice a day: once in the morning and once in the afternoon. A session is a morning or afternoon period in a school.

How the attendance rate is calculated

Attendance rate is worked out by adding together the sessions that all pupils have attended. This number is then divided by the number of possible sessions that all pupils could have attended. The result is multiplied by 100 to get a percentage.

Persistent absence

2024 to 2025 academic year

If a pupil misses 10% or more of their possible sessions in a year, they are identified as being persistently absent.

On average there are 380 sessions a year in school, so a pupil who misses 38 or more sessions is considered persistently absent.

What you could ask schools about attendance

When researching, contacting or visiting a school, you may want to ask:

  • what does the school do to encourage regular attendance?
  • how does the school work with families to support attendance?
  • how does the school support pupils with medical needs that may lead to absence?
