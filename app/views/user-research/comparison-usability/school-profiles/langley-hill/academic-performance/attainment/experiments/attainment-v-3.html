Attainment

See how well pupils perform in their GCSEs compared with local authority and national averages by comparing ‘Attainment 8’ scores.

Attainment 8 is an average of pupils’ results across different subject groupings.

Learn more about Attainment 8

The 8 subjects that are used in Attainment 8 are split into 3 groups:

  • English and maths, which are both double-weighted
  • EBacc subjects, these are a group of GCSE subjects that help students gain a broad set of knowledge and skills. These subjects include sciences, geography, history, and languages
  • open group which includes any other approved GCSEs or vocational qualifications

Each GCSE grade is given a point score (for example grade 9 = 9 points, grade 8 = 8 points, and so on). The scores from the best 8 subjects (with English and Maths counting twice) are added together to give the Average score across 8 subjects (Attainment 8).

What this means

A score of 45 means the average pupil achieved GCSE grades of around 4 to 5 across their subjects. This is a standard to strong pass.

This score is 5 points lower than the local authority average. This means pupils at this school perform below other schools locally.

The score is 5 points higher than the national average. This means pupils perform better than the national average.
