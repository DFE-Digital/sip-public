Explore more information

You can find out more about each school you're comparing by viewing the school profile or visiting the school website.

Arrange a school visit

Visiting a school allows you to experience what the school is really like. You can observe the learning environment, meet the staff, and explore whether it feels right for your child.

Most schools run open days, or you can contact them to arrange a visit and ask questions. This can be especially helpful if your child has additional needs and you want to understand more about the transition to secondary school.

Applying to a school

If you have chosen a school, you can review the schools admissions policy and apply through the local council’s secondary school application process.

You can see our the admissions guidance and explore more about the schools on their individual profiles.
