Staying in education, employment and apprenticeships

Pupil destinations is a measure of what pupils go on to do after finishing Key stage 4 (KS4). Pupils usually finish KS4 after year 11 when they are 16. Between the ages of 16 and 18 they must be:

  • in full-time education, for example at a college or sixth form
  • starting an apprenticeship
  • spending 20 hours or more a week working or volunteering, while in part-time education or training

What you could ask schools about pupil destinations

When researching, contacting or visiting a school, you may want to ask:

  • how the school supports pupils with their post-16 choices
  • what work experience is available through the school?
  • what career advice or support is available?
  • will subject choice affect future applications?
